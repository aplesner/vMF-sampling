What is pre-registered (summary)

  • Estimand: band width Wτ in decades, referenced to each arm's own sweep peak — immune to the undertuned-baseline attack by construction, and survives an accuracy null.
  • Contrasts: normalization = W(DeLoRA)−W(LoRA); per-component delta = W(PC)−W(DeLoRA). PC−LoRA is explicitly not primary.
  • Noise floor: the r=1 null (PC ≡ DeLoRA) runs at every tier; its spread defines "zero".
  • Pinned protocol: AdamW wd=0, cosine, warmup 2%, batch 32, α=2r, no grad accumulation — identical across arms.
  • Do not run: Appendix-A singular-value-dispersion experiment (Theorem 1 false: for σ=(10,1) it predicts error 40.5, actual minimum is 0.0). AUC-under-LR-curve not primary. commonsense170k dropped as a substrate.

Implementation corrections to the thesis, recorded

  • Thesis claims si ≥ 0 in three places; with softplus(λ)−log 2 the range is (−log 2, ∞). Replicated verbatim (asymmetric); a sign-symmetric variant is flagged as the cleaner control for sign freedom, not yet an arm.
  • Thesis's disjoint LR grids were correct argmax protocol but structurally cannot measure band edges; the common grid replaces them, and LoRA divergence points are kept as data.
  • The buried one-sentence robustness claim (App. B.1: DoLoRA stable where LoRA/DoRA diverge) is promoted to the headline estimand with a pre-registered divergence criterion.
